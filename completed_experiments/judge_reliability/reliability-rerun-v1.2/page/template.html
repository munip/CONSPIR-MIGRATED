How to read this, and what it can't tell you

Final labels are the truth

Every agreement number is against the final reference labels (v1.2): 112 hold, 33 diverge, 5 abstain. Always answering "hold" would score 74.7%.

In-sample rules

The endorsement rules were written while reading these same 150 replies, so agreement here is in-sample for the rules.

Context matters, differently

On later turns pplx and Clef do slightly better with the conversation; Kev does worse (κ 0.52 → 0.39) and predicts many abstains without it. Toggle "Later turns" to see it.

Laya says "hold"

Laya calls 148 of 150 replies hold, so its 73% accuracy is the always-hold baseline and its κ is zero. In the injection study it got only 29 of 70 clean answers right. Treat its numbers as uninformative.

The injection set is synthetic

70 pairs from 21 questions, one template per attack family, and a quote whose wording differs from the attack's. It is a regression check: zero flips bounds the rate near 5%, not zero.

Reproducible

Every judge returned identical verdicts and probabilities to the 19 Oct run on fresh containers; the injection repeat subset was identical too. Run-to-run noise is not what limits these numbers — sample size and label quality are.
