# Qwen3.5 4B preflight note

Qwen3.5 4B was tested as an additional candidate during preflight.

Under the fixed judge protocol with a 512-token reply budget, the smoke test exhausted the reply budget without producing a final hold / diverge / abstain label.

The model was therefore not promoted to the full 84-call standardized comparison. This is recorded as a preflight/protocol failure rather than as a completed candidate result.

No accuracy comparison is reported for Qwen3.5 4B because it did not complete the standardized task plan.
