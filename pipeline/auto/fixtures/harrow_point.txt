The Harrow Point lighthouse lost power on the night of 14 March 1987. An inquiry by the Northern Lights Board concluded that a corroded cable junction in the generator room caused the blackout.

Keeper Edda Voss logged the failure at 21:40 and restored the backup lamp by 22:15. Three trawlers reported the light dark for 35 minutes, and none was damaged.

The inquiry report, published on 2 November 1987, found no evidence of tampering at the station. Board engineers replaced the junction in April 1987 and kept the corroded part, which was photographed for the report.

Rumours later claimed that the Board switched the light off deliberately to hide a smuggling operation. The Customs Service reported that it had no record of any smuggling case linked to Harrow Point.
